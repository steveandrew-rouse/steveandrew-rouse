# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes anyone who wants to improve the profile, report a broken link, or point out something inaccurate. Participation is open regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and generous about the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and ask why rather than relitigate.
- Give credit where it is due.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sustained disruption in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing a report of misconduct as a joke or an overreaction.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to Steve Andrew Rouse through the contact links on the profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or block accounts as needed.
